## Becoming a Maintainer

Maintainers are contributors who have shown sustained, high-quality
contributions and good judgement, and who have been nominated by an existing
maintainer. Reviewers and contributors are organised in the
`kubernetes-csi-addons-reviewers` and `kubernetes-csi-addons-contributors`
GitHub teams, which are used for requesting and approving reviews (see
[`.github/mergify.yml`](.github/mergify.yml)).

Please see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for details on how to contribute.
